# Sol RO3: events plan

*Written 22 Oct 2026. Companion plans: [strategy](balance-retention-strategy.md) · [roadmap](roadmap.md). Dates are
proposals; times are in **realm time**, and "prime time" means the realm's busiest evening hours (check the
login graph, and assume about 20:00 until then).*

## 1. Rules for every event

1. **A rhythm players can learn.** The same thing on the same weekday at the same time. A predictable week beats
   surprise events, because players plan around it and bring friends.
2. **Cheap to run.** Each event should take one GM about 30–60 minutes. Anything that needs more becomes a
   tooling item on the [roadmap](roadmap.md) first.
3. **Rewards are mostly cosmetic or convenience.** Titles, outfits, emotes, Bonus Time credit, consumables, Zeny
   sinks. No refine ore at scale, no top gear, and no Crystals beyond the F2P budget. Reward showing up as well as
   winning.
4. **Light on FOMO.** Seasonal cosmetics come back the next year, or can be bought later with event tokens. Missing
   one weekend should never make a player feel behind.
5. **Announce everywhere.** Discord `#announcements` 24 hours and 1 hour before, then in game with `!announce`
   15 minutes before and at the start. Post winners and screenshots afterwards in `#general` and `#screenshots`.
6. **Measure it.** Note the attendance, how long it took, and one thing to change. Drop events that stay under
   about 5 players twice in a row, and grow the ones that fill up.

## 2. Weekly rhythm (from the first week of November)

Built-in activities already run on their own clocks: Commendation Vault (10:00–22:00 daily, level 44+), Festival
Invitation quiz, Prontera Defense, the guild campfire, Guild Challenge and Hunting Challenge, MVP spawns, Territory
Wars every 14 days, and the 05:00 daily reset. The weekly plan *highlights* one of them each night so the whole
population turns up together, and adds a GM layer on top.

| Night (prime time) | Theme | What happens | Tools |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | Guild Night | Guild Challenge / Hunting Challenge push; weekly guild ranking posted on Discord | Built-in; leaderboards |
| **Tuesday** | MVP Hunt | 2–3 MVPs announced 15 minutes ahead in one area; the top contributors get shout-outs | `!mvp spawn`, `!announce` |
| **Wednesday** | Arena Night | Survivor Battle and Dragon Coffin Island queues at a fixed hour (needs the small-population match sizes, roadmap P1) | Built-in + house rule |
| **Thursday** | Community Night | GM event from the rotation in §3 (Find the GM, trivia, treasure hunt, fashion show) | `!warp`, `!jump`, `!announce` |
| **Friday** | Party Night | A `#party-finder` LFG ping at a fixed hour; GMs and volunteers fill dungeon parties so nobody is left out (RO3's "5–10 player dungeons" are its most-cited barrier); a screenshot theme of the week on Discord | Discord; smaller party minimum (roadmap 1.11) |
| **Saturday** | War Night | Territory Wars on its 14-day cycle; on the other Saturdays, Guild League or a guild-vs-guild scrim | Built-in |
| **Sunday** | Bonus Sunday | A Bonus Time / EXP window (once the event-rate tool is in, roadmap P1); the weekly recap and patch notes | Tooling |

**Daily:** a "first MVP of the day" Crystal reward (already in F2P rules) and the pass daily challenges. Keep the
dailies **short**, about 20 minutes. Lost Ark shows what happens when dailies become chores.

## 3. GM event rotation (runs with tools that exist today)

Until the gift tool lands (roadmap P1), GMs give prizes by **trade** in game, and every winner gets a Discord
shout-out.

| Event | How to run it | Prize idea |
|---|---|---|
| **Find the GM** (a classic RO event) | The GM hides on a map (`!warp`, `!jump`), then posts 3 hints 5 minutes apart with `!announce`. The first 3 to reach the GM and `/emote` win. | A cosmetic, consumables, a title shout-out |
| **MVP Invasion** | `!mvp spawn` 3–5 MVPs on a field next to town. Everyone fights together, and contribution counts more than the last hit. | MVP drops as usual + a group photo |
| **RO Trivia** | 10 questions in world chat (RO1 lore, RO3 maps, monster names). The first correct answer scores a point; the top 3 win. | Zeny, consumables |
| **Treasure Hunt** | Discord posts a riddle that points to a map and coordinates; the GM waits there. Answers in game only. | A rare cosmetic |
| **Fashion Show** | Meet in Prontera with a theme (for example "all black" or "Payon villager"); players vote on Discord within 24 hours. | Fashion tab item, a "Trendsetter" title on Discord |
| **World Auction Night** | `!worldauction open <items>` with cosmetic or limited items. **This doubles as a Zeny sink.** | Bidders pay Zeny |
| **Guild Boss Rush** | Guilds sign up, the GM spawns one MVP per guild in turn, and the fastest kill wins (timed on stream or Discord). | A guild cosmetic or a guild-wide consumable pack |
| **Build Contest** | Players post a build or guide in `#builds-and-guides`; the community votes; the winner is pinned. | Consumables + recognition |
| **Pet Fashion Show** *(from Reddit)* | Bring your pet to Prontera; themes like "smallest pet" or "best pet + outfit combo"; Discord vote | A pet cosmetic or treats |
| **Novice Survival** *(from Reddit, "my favorite")* | Everyone makes a fresh level 1 character with nothing. They meet in a small field, the GM spawns something deadly, and the last novice standing wins. Needs one test run to pick a map and a monster that `!mvp spawn` can place | A cosmetic for the winner and the last 3 |
| **Roleplay Quest** *(from Reddit)* | The GM plays an NPC with a story ("the Payon orphanage needs repairs, gather materials"), the players collect items, and they hand them in by trade. Improvised, D&D style | Zeny + a title shout-out |
| **Town Chill Night** *(from Reddit)* | A tea party in Prontera: sit, emote, chat, and take a group photo, with a surprise MVP spawn mid-way ("someone accidentally summoning an MVP, and all hell breaks loose" is the most-upvoted RO memory in the cozy thread) | The MVP's drops |
| **Duel Tournament** | A monthly 1v1 and 2v2 bracket on a PvP map, run on Discord. Small-format PvP is the kind that still works in RO | A title, Competition Medals |
| **Poring Pop** *(from Reddit; needs the spawn tool, roadmap 2.11)* | The GM floods a field with weak monsters; a few marked ones drop the prize | Whatever is inside |

Rotate these on Community Night: weeks 1–4 = Find the GM → Trivia → Treasure Hunt → Fashion Show, weeks 5–8 = Novice
Survival → Pet Fashion Show → Roleplay Quest → Town Chill Night, then repeat with substitutions.

## 5. Programmes that run all year

### New players
- **First-hour path.** A pinned Discord guide (`#install-guide` → `#help`) plus an in-game welcome mail with the
  path to Base 40 and the job change.
- **Mentor bonus.** A player of Base 70+ who parties with a newcomer up to their job change earns a mentor token.
  Both get a cosmetic when the newcomer reaches Base 40. (A Discord "Mentor" role first, the in-game tracking later.)
- **Bring a friend.** Word of mouth is what grows RO servers. When a referred friend reaches Base 40, both get a
  cosmetic and Bonus Time credit.

### Returners (14 or 30 days away)
RO players "don't quit, they take extended breaks" (57 upvotes on r/RagnarokOnline), so expect returners and make
their way back easy.
- A **welcome-back kit** on login: Bonus Time credit, a consumable pack, a returner cosmetic, and a "what changed
  while you were away" mail linking the patch notes (Black Desert-style comeback kit).
- Catch-up EXP for levels below the current cap minus 10 (see the strategy).

### Ongoing competition
- **Monthly ladders:** MVP Slayer, Arena (Survivor/Dragon), Training DPS by class, Guild of the Month. Titles last a
  month; the winners are posted on Discord.
- **Hall of fame:** the first to each cap per class, the first guild to hold a castle, and the first MVP kills.
  It costs nothing and players remember it for years.

## 6. Community and Discord

- **`#suggestions`:** a monthly "Suggestion of the Month" vote among the top 5. The winner is built in the following
  month and credited in the patch notes, so players can see their ideas get built.
- **`#bug-reports`:** stays with the bug automation; humans don't edit its threads (see the roadmap lane rules).
- **`#patch-notes`** weekly, **`#dev-log`** for work in progress, and a monthly **roadmap post**.
- **Player council:** 5–7 active players from different guilds and classes. A monthly voice or text call before each
  balance patch.
- **The 30-day survey (Google Form):** why you play, what you'd change, what nearly made you quit, prime-time
  hours, and favourite events. Reddit tells us what RO players in general want; this tells us what our players want.
- **Write announcements in your own voice.** Reddit readers spot and dismiss AI-written server posts. Use the bug
  automation's patch-notes drafts as notes, then rewrite them before posting.
- **Event reminders:** the existing `solro3-auto` box can post the scheduled reminders in §1.5 (as its own job, kept
  apart from bug triage).

## 7. Reward guidelines

| Give freely | Give carefully | Don't give |
|---|---|---|
| Titles, outfits, emotes, profile stickers, group photos, Discord roles | Bonus Time credit, consumables, Zeny (small), Competition Medals, event tokens | Blessed Ores or refine material at scale, top-tier gear, cards that set the meta, Crystals beyond the F2P weekly budget, anything only Supporters can get that has stats |
